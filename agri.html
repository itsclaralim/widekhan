Quality & Traceability

Food-chain cargo carries food-chain obligations.

Agricultural cargo can fail on things a chemical shipment never will — a pest finding, a mycotoxin result, a phytosanitary certificate issued in the wrong format. We plan for those before loading.

Phytosanitary & health certification

Certificates issued by the origin authority in the format the destination quarantine service accepts.

Independent quality analysis

Moisture, protein, foreign matter, aflatoxin and pesticide residue tested at load port by an accredited laboratory.

Origin traceability

Crop year, origin region and processing facility recorded so the lot can be traced back if a claim arises.

Certification schemes

Halal, Kosher, non-GMO, organic and sustainability certification arranged where the buyer's market requires it.
