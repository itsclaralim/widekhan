Quality & Safety

Chemicals are unforgiving of shortcuts.

A hazardous cargo with a wrong UN number or a missing SDS does not just get delayed — it gets refused. We treat documentation as part of the product.

Certificate of Analysis on every lot

Batch-linked CoA issued against the contracted specification, released before shipment.

Dangerous goods compliance

IMDG classification, UN-approved packing, correct marking and DG declarations prepared for the carrier.

SDS in the destination format

Safety data sheets supplied in the language and regulatory format required at the receiving country.

Third-party inspection

Pre-shipment sampling and analysis through SGS, Intertek or an inspector you nominate.

Packing & Delivery

Delivered in the format your plant handles.

ISO TANK

Bulk liquid

ISO tank containers and flexitanks for solvent and glycol volumes where drum handling is not economic.

DRUM / IBC

Packed liquid

200 L steel or HDPE drums and 1,000 L IBCs, UN-approved where the classification requires it.

BAG / BIG BAG

Solids

25 kg bags on shrink-wrapped pallets, or 500–1,250 kg big bags for volume powder movements.

CUSTOM

Repacking

Neutral or private-label packing, customer-specified labelling and re-export configurations.
