How We Work

From enquiry to discharge.

A single, documented process — so you always know which stage your cargo is at.

Enquiry & specification

You send the product, grade, target volume and destination. We confirm what is technically and commercially achievable before quoting.

Sourcing & sample approval

We match the requirement to qualified producers, issue an offer with full specification, and arrange samples for your approval where required.

Contract & payment terms

Sales contract issued with Incoterms, packing, inspection basis and payment structure — L/C, T/T or D/P — agreed in writing.

Production & quality control

Production monitored against the contract specification. Third-party inspection (SGS, Intertek and equivalent) arranged on request.

Shipping & documentation

Booking, packing, hazardous classification where applicable, and the full document set released against agreed terms.

Arrival & after-sales

Arrival tracked to discharge, with claims, repeat-order scheduling and forward contracting handled by the same team.
