How We Plug In

Four ways customers use us.

MODE 01

Primary supplier

We hold the annual contract for a raw material and manage specification, price basis and call-off schedule against your production plan.

MODE 02

Second source

We qualify as a backup to your incumbent — same specification, independent supply chain — so a plant outage upstream does not stop your line.

MODE 03

Spot & project buying

One-off volumes, new-product trials and project cargoes — quoted quickly with the same documentation discipline as term business.

MODE 04

Ex-stock purchase

For products we hold in inventory, quote, contract and dispatch happen in days — buying without the international lead time.
